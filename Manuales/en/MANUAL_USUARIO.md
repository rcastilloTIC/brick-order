## How to use this manual
Follow the chapters in order during your first visit. Actions may only become available when the administrator opens the relevant campaign phase. Visible labels can vary according to the selected language and yearly configuration.

**This handbook is for participants, not administrators.** It does not describe privileged features or methods of bypassing approval. If a screen differs, verify the active campaign and contact the organiser.

## 1. What VALBRICK is - and is not
VALBRICK coordinates collective LEGO-part orders: catalogue, proposed quantities, selection of references, purchase commitments and final documents. **A vote is not merely an opinion poll:** it represents a commitment under the campaign rules.

- **User identity**: your login and password, which may persist across campaigns.
- **Yearly participation**: approval for a specific campaign, with a signed document and separate review.
- **Product**: a reference and colour, with a price and quantity rules.
- **Vote / commitment**: a committed quantity during the voting phases.
- **Effective order**: a materialised quantity for a finally selected product, used for the final export.

Not every voted-for reference is necessarily included in the final order. Always check the published status.

## 2. First access, language and security
1. Open the HTTPS address given by the organiser. If a site-access password page appears, enter the shared site passphrase: **this is not your personal password**.
2. Choose **Log in** and enter your username and password. Contact the administrator if your account is disabled or unknown.
3. You may be required to change the initial password. Choose a unique, long password. The form enforces **at least eight characters**; longer is safer.
4. Use your account menu to open **Profile**, update your details or log out.
5. From the top bar choose **ES / EN** and appearance (**automatic, light or dark**). These settings do not modify amounts or votes.

**Privacy:** do not share screenshots of your profile, national ID, signed PDF, password or payment details. Log out on shared computers.

## 3. Annual enrolment and signed document
1. The administrator must enrol you in the active yearly campaign. Having an account from a previous year is not sufficient.
2. When directed to the commitment screen, download the **campaign commitment PDF** for that year.
3. Read, complete and sign it according to the organiser's instructions. Keep your own copy.
4. On **Upload your signed declaration**, select your PDF. The application checks the extension and PDF header; that does not replace human review of its content.
5. Wait on the **declaration pending** screen until the administrator approves it. If rejected, follow the organiser's corrections and upload a replacement.
6. If the commitment document is unavailable, contact the administrator rather than reusing an older year's document.

**Profile:** the application accepts name, surname, Spanish ID (DNI/NIE) and age. The form rejects invalid DNI/NIE checks and an entered age under 14. Do not use fictional identity details in a real campaign.

## 4. Finding your way around
The **Dashboard** displays the catalogue and phase-dependent controls. Filter by name, colour and product groups; choose the pieces or order view, check favourites and inspect rankings when available. A **favourite** is a personal bookmark: it is not an order and does not replace a vote.

The **Insights** page offers visible campaign indicators. Administration, imports and global exports are restricted to organisers. Some images may load externally, and some references have no image; identify an item by reference and colour rather than relying only on a picture.

## 5. Entering a quantity
1. Find the reference and verify **reference code, colour, name and price**. Visually similar pieces can be distinct products.
2. Enter the quantity in the voting control and submit it. Wait for confirmation and check the quantity shown afterwards.
3. Current step rules: **prices at or below EUR 1.35: minimum 25 in multiples of 25**; **prices above EUR 1.35: minimum 10 in multiples of 10**. These are code rules, not a price list or a guarantee for future campaigns.
4. Examples: a EUR 1.00 piece allows 25, 50 or 75 units; 30 is invalid. A EUR 2.00 piece allows 10, 20 or 30; 25 is invalid.
5. The system also checks budgets, campaign phase, permissions, eligible products and protected committed quantities. **Do not assume commitments can be reduced**; restrictions may prevent such changes.

## 6. Campaign phases
| Phase | What happens | Your action |
|---|---|---|
| `first` | Initial voting and proposals under personal limits. | Review the catalogue and commitments. |
| `second` | Second selection window, with reference limits. | Follow messages about eligible products. |
| `final` | Ranking is consolidated and ties may require review. | Check the finally selected products. |
| `order` | Selected commitments become effective orders. | Verify your summary and read notices. |

| **Public shortage notice** | During `order`, the administrator may open an extraordinary increase window if the minimum total has not been reached. | Acknowledge the notice before adding quantities. |

## 7. Public-only procedure: insufficient collective order
If the collective order falls below the minimum, the administrator may open an **extraordinary window lasting 1 to 30 days**. The dashboard displays a notice. First choose **Acknowledge** to confirm you have read it; then open **Increase my order** while the window remains open. Only finally selected products and valid quantities are permitted. Each addition increases the effective order; this is not a new general voting round.

A deadline passing does not by itself trigger automatic cancellation: the administrator must reconcile the state (or run the scheduled maintenance action). Once the minimum is reached the window closes as reached; if it expires below the minimum, cancellation for shortage may follow. Archived campaigns retain their historical rows.

**The Public edition has no private invitations or Extended rounds.** Do not follow procedures written for the other edition.

## 8. Final order and downloads
Once the organiser generates the final order, check your summary. The application offers an individual download from the dashboard (`/dashboard/order-pdf`) when the per-user PDF bundle exists. The global Excel and other export files are administrative and may contain other people's data; do not share them indiscriminately.

For each line verify **reference, colour, units and price**. Shipping and tax, when configured, appear on the corresponding documents. Payment details are set by the administrator; do not transfer money to bank details received in unverified messages.

## 9. History and future campaigns
After archiving, the order becomes part of historical records. Rows with existing orders cannot simply be deleted or edited. Administrators may prepare a draft for the next campaign, but participation and the signed document must be checked for each year. Historical data is not an active order.

## 10. Frequently asked questions and troubleshooting
| Situation | What to check |
|---|---|
| I cannot log in. | Check the site passphrase, then your personal login; administrators can confirm account status. |
| I keep seeing the password form. | Complete the mandatory first-password change. |
| My document is pending. | The PDF needs review and annual approval by an administrator. |
| My PDF is rejected. | Submit a genuine PDF within the upload limit; renaming the extension is not enough. |
| My quantity is rejected. | Check the product price, 25/10 steps, budget, phase and product eligibility. |
| A voted piece is absent from the order. | It may have been excluded during final selection or disabled. |
| I cannot reduce my quantity. | Purchase commitments are protected; ask your organiser to explain the policy. |
| Payment document unavailable. | The final order may not have been generated yet. |
| New year, no voting access. | Enrolment and document approval are annual. |

## 11. Good practices before confirming
- Check the campaign year and title.
- Identify products by code, colour and units rather than image alone.
- Keep a copy of accepted rules and your final downloads.
- Raise discrepancies **before** final closure. Removing a favourite does not cancel a commitment.
- Protect your account and personal documents.

## 12. Basic glossary
**Campaign:** registered annual period. **Participation:** an individual's entitlement for that year. **Commitment:** voted quantity subject to the process rules. **Final selection:** references chosen according to ranking rules. **Materialisation:** transfer of selected quantities into effective orders. **Archive:** preserved closed campaign. **E2E:** automated end-to-end user journey test.

## Appendix. Reporting a problem usefully
Tell the administrator (without secrets) the year, page or phase, product reference, exact error message, selected language and action attempted. This makes an issue reproducible without sharing passwords or signed PDFs.
